March 29, 2025

Spring Break, Collaborative Concert and More

Spring has arrived, bringing cherry blossoms and tulips—even in the chilly weather! We have a few important announcements to share with you:

Dear NWCC Family,

Spring has arrived, bringing cherry blossoms and tulips—even in the chilly weather! We have a few important announcements to share with you:

1. Spring Break (April 5 & 12, 2025)

We will observe Spring Break on April 5th and 12th. During these two weeks, there will be no chamber, choir, enrichment programs, or music lessons. Enjoy a restful break, and we’ll see you back on April 19th!

2. Collaborative Concert (Chamber) – April 19 & 20, 2025

Music Director Sung Lee has invited NWCC musicians to collaborate on a special performance for the Easter Mass at Our Lady of Fatima. Students who participate will earn extra service hours.

April 19 (Saturday), 6:30 – 10:00 PM

April 20 (Sunday), 8:00 – 10:00 AM

Interested students will travel to Our Lady of Fatima with Mr. Chung after our regular NWCC practice. More details will be shared soon.

3. Future Plans

May: We plan to host a community concert at a nursing home. If you know of a facility that would welcome an NWCC performance, please reach out!

June: Our final NWCC fundraising concert is in the works! We are considering an outdoor venue or a concert hall and will share details soon.

Celebrating Achievements

We are proud of our NWCC musicians who have won competitions or been accepted into universities. Congratulations! Thank you for sharing your successes—they inspire our community.

Together, we continue our mission: Bringing Music and Education to All. Thank you for your dedication and support!
