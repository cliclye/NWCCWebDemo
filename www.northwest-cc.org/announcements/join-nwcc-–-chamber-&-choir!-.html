Join NWCC – Chamber & Choir!

Northwest Collaborative Center (NWCC) is a non-profit organization in the Seattle area, bringing together students passionate about music and education through meaningful volunteer opportunities.

We are now recruiting members for the 2025–2026 season!

Chamber & Choir Opportunities

Chamber

Open to middle and high school students playing violin, viola, or cello.

Choir

Open to choir enthusiasts from 5th grade to high school.

Location: Kirkland Covenant Church

Annual Donation: $500
