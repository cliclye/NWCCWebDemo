Easter Mass at Our Lady of Fatima

Thank you to everyone who reached out about the Easter Mass concert at Our Lady of Fatima. We had six studentsparticipate in this special collaboration, and we were excited to see them work with other musicians for a shared cause. We’ll be sharing photos and videos of their performance soon!

Upcoming Events & Important Updates

1. Spring Break (April 5 & 12, 2025)

NWCC will be on Spring Break for two weeks (April 5 & 12). There will be no chamber, choir, enrichment programs, or music lessons during this time. Enjoy a restful break, and we’ll see you back on April 19th when all programs resume as usual.

2. Updated Schedule for April 19

Since we’ll be attending the Easter Mass at Our Lady of Fatima, NWCC practice will end early at 6:00 PM. No music lessons or sectionals will be held on this day. Please plan to pick up musicians by 6:00 PM. Regular scheduling will resume on April 26.

3. Community Concert at Kirkland Central Church (May 4, 2025)

NWCC’s Chamber and Choir have been invited to perform at Kirkland Central Church, where we regularly hold practice. We will perform three pieces during the Sunday Service. Afterward, the church community will provide a warm meal for our musicians.

Date: Sunday, May 4, 2025 Location: Kirkland Central Church Arrival: 10:30 AM (Education Hall) Service Begins: 11:00 AM Dress Code: Black & White, formal attire

Please confirm your availability by filling out the form: Sign-Up Form

4. Future Plans

May: We plan to host a community concert at a nursing home. If you know of a facility that would welcome an NWCC performance, please let us know!

June: Our final fundraising concert is in the works! We’re considering an outdoor venue or a concert hall and will provide more details soon.

Next Year (2025-26): NWCC has already received invitations to perform at various community events, including the WABA Korea 2025 Expo! Our group is gaining recognition, and we encourage all musicians to continue honing their skills for these exciting opportunities. For reference, check out last year’s WABA Korea 2024: WABA Expo

5. Celebrating Achievements

We are incredibly proud of our NWCC musicians who have won competitions or been accepted into universities! Your hard work and dedication inspire our entire community. Congratulations!

Continuing Our Mission

Together, we remain committed to Bringing Music and Education to All. Thank you for being part of NWCC—we appreciate your dedication and support!
