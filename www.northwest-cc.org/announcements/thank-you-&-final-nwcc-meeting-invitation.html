June 13, 2025

Thank You & Final NWCC Meeting Invitation

First, We will have NWCC Final Meeting at 5-6pm this Saturday (6/14/25)

Dear NWCC Families,

First, We will have NWCC Final Meeting at 5-6pm this Saturday (6/14/25)

We will have a grand closing meeting with all chamber, choir, and enrichment program groups together. So, no separate hours for the programs, and no practice! Come and check what we've accomplished and how many service hours you've earned. ^^

It will be a time for recognition, reflection, and celebration!

Thank you for making our final concert such a memorable success! Thanks to your generosity, we raised $611 during the event. We will keep the donation period open for two more weeks. Any contributions marked with “Summer Gift,” “Cocoon House,” or “NWCC Final Concert” will be added to our final donation total for Cocoon House. Checks can be made out to Northwest Collaborative Center.

Or write a check to Northwest Collaborative Center

Special thanks to Taiwoo, father of Cheon-hee and Yeon-hee, for beautifully capturing the concert through photos and video. He has launched an NWCC YouTube channel, where you can now relive our performances and share them with friends and family. Please visit the channel, click “Like,” and help us grow our audience and impact!

https://youtu.be/OuAMlz4Emrc

We are excited to welcome Christina Cuenca from Cocoon House, who will share more about their mission and how our students can begin building relationships with social organizations that support youth in need. NWCC students will also take a commemorative photo with Christina to mark this special collaboration.

Parents are warmly invited to stay and celebrate with us. During this gathering, we will also present a few awards to recognize students who have shown outstanding dedication this season.

Thank you again for walking with us on this meaningful journey. Your support has made all the difference.

With gratitude,

Jeongwon
