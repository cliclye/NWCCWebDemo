December 2025 Update

Happy Holidays! This is a season of gratitude, and I want to express my sincere appreciation for all of you. Thank you for your continued collaboration and support of NWCC.

Dear NWCC Families,

Happy Holidays! This is a season of gratitude, and I want to express my sincere appreciation for all of you. Thank you for your continued collaboration and support of NWCC.

Our focus is not only on musical excellence but also on discovering ways to serve our community through music. Aligned with our mission, “Music and Education for All,” we are very proud to offer meaningful opportunities for our young musicians to collaborate and give back this year.

More recently, we also performed at the Our Lady of Fatima. See the link to Youtube (below) to see the example of the performance with NWCC and Northwest Seattle Catholic Choir!

Our Lady of Fatima - is a beautiful church located in Seattle, and NWCC got the opportunity to experience the power of collaboration with Northwest Seattle Catholic Choir, Ultimately creating beautiful music. The music director of the Our Lady of Fatima, highly values our students and is truly grateful for this opportunity.

NWCC students once again feels proud of the music they create and understand the impact their contributions have on the community. It was wonderful to see the collaborative work between NWCC young musicians and Fatima Choir.
