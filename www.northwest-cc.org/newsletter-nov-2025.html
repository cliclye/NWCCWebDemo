November 2025 News Letter

Northwest Collaborative Chamber Newsletter

Time flies, and it is already the end of October.

I hope all NWCC families are healthy and happy.

We have several important announcements in this newsletter.

1. Upcoming Performance

We are thrilled to announce the start of our Community Concert Series! Thank you all for your continued dedication and musicianship.

November 15th (Saturday) at 4pm - Performance at Ages Living Bellevue

Address: 148 102nd Ave SE Bellevue WA 98004

Please arrive by 3:30pm for setup and tuning.

Dress code: Black and white formal attire.

Bring the music and music stand.

This is our first official community concert of the 2025-26 season.

Students will earn 3 service hours for this performance.

We are delighted to return to this venue after last Summer’s wonderful concert - they invited us back with great excitement!

November 22nd at 6:30pm - Rehearsal practice at Our Lady of Fatima

Address: 3218 West Barrett st. Seattle, WA 98199

Please arrive by 6:15pm to the site for tuning.

This rehearsal is in preparation for our December performance.

Due to the Thanksgiving holiday, rehearsal will be held on-site on the 22nd.

Students will earn 3 service hours for this rehearsal practice.

We’re happy to collaborate again with Our Lady of Fatima, who hosted us for the Easter concert last season.

December 4th (Thur) at 7pm Performance at Our Lady of Fatima

Address: 3218 West Barrett st. Seattle, WA 98199

Please arrive by 5:30pm for the dress rehearsal.

Dress code: Black and white formal attire.

Bring your music and music stand.

This is a collaborative fundraising concert with Our Lady of Fatima Parish.

Students will earn 5 service hours for this performance.

We are excited to return after last season’s Easter concert!

No regular practice on Dec. 6th (Sat) as we performed at Our Lady Fatima Parish on 4th (Thur).

No music lessons on November 15th (Sat), November 22nd (Sat), and December 6th (Sat) as we are not meeting at our regular practice location.

2. Community Outreach

The NWCC Outreach Team (Gracy, Jun, Kaila, Rachel and myself) has been diligently connecting with various community organizations to arrange our community concerts and fundraising events.

Applause to our NWCC students for their leadership and dedication in sharing their talents with the community!

3. Financial Update

The new NWCC bank account is now fully set up!

We transitioned to the new account due to a security issue, and all checks and annual donations have been successfully deposited.

Donation receipts will be sent by mid November.

If you have not received your receipt by Nov. 22nd, please contact me directly to northwestcc.hyun@gmail.com.

This is the new QR code that works with NWCC.

If you haven’t yet submitted your annual donation for the 2025-26 season, please use this new QR code to send it through your bank account. We appreciate your support!

4. Open to New Musicians

NWCC is always welcoming new young musicians who wish to share their talent and serve the community through music.

If you have friends and classmates who might be interested, please invite them to join our NWCC chamber practice and performances!
