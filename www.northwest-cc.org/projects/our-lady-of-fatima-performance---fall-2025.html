Performance and Rehearsal at Our Lady of Fatima

Our Lady of Fatima Performance - Fall 2025

Our Lady of Fatima - is a beautiful church located in Seattle, and NWCC got the opportunity to experience the power of collaboration with Northwest Seattle Catholic Choir, Ultimately creating beautiful music. The music director of the Our Lady of Fatima, highly values our students and is truly grateful for this opportunity.

NWCC students once again feels proud of the music they create and understand the impact their contributions have on the community. It was wonderful to see the collaborative work between NWCC young musicians and Fatima Choir.

Here are links to a couple of examples of music played at the performance!
