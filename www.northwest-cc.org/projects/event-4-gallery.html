Concert at Kirkland Central Church - Spring 2025

Community Concert at Kirkland Central Church

We were honored to have our Chamber Orchestra and Choir invited to perform at Kirkland Central Church for a special concert event. It was a memorable and meaningful experience for all of us, as we had the opportunity to share our music in a warm and welcoming setting. During the concert, we performed three carefully chosen pieces: "Lord, I Need You", "Edelweiss", and "The Lord’s Prayer". Each piece was performed with dedication and passion, and we were grateful for the chance to connect with the audience through our music.

May 4th, 2025 - Kirkland Central Church, 14220 Juanita Woodinville Way NE, Kirkland, WA 98034, USA
