About Us

Who are we?

Northwest Collaborative Center (NWCC) is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization based in Kirkland, Washington, serving the surrounding regions.

We are an all-volunteer organization, funded entirely by public donations. NWCC is committed to increasing educational access for socioeconomically disadvantaged youth, primarily through music. We provide opportunities for these young individuals to engage in music and collaborate with fellow youth musicians.

By equipping our local communities with resources and tools, we aim to foster positive social change. We believe that through the power of music and education, we can cultivate stronger families and healthier communities.

NWCC WORKS TO IMPROVE EDUCATIONAL EQUITY

OUR ACTIVITIES

We offer weekly music instrument lessons for young people, allowing them to practice individually or as part of a chamber. Each session typically lasts 1-2 hours, during which we emphasize the importance of sharing talents and fostering gratitude through music.

Many of our program participants go on to volunteer, helping to teach new students. Building on this success, we are expanding our offerings to include an academic aid program. This initiative is designed to support underprivileged students who face barriers to learning due to financial limitations.

Currently, we are developing a curriculum for core subjects to provide these students with academic resources, empowering them to achieve their educational goals.

Our Purpose

Our chamber is dedicated to serving the community by performing for non-profit organizations and hosting fundraising events to support local causes.

We believe that music should be accessible to everyone, regardless of who you are. We provide music lessons for youth and teens including those with and without financial needs or disabilities. Our inclusive environment allows all participants to express themselves, connect with others, and contribute to musical ensembles.

Through the transformative power of music, we aim to inspire personal growth, foster community involvement, and enrich the lives of all children.
